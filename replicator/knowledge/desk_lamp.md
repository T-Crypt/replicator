title: Balanced-arm desk lamp
keywords: desk lamp, task lamp, balanced arm, balanced-arm, anglepoise, architect lamp, reading lamp, lamp
rise: arm 35-80
---
Orientation: Y is up, the base sits on the desk at y = 0, and the lamp reaches forward over the work surface in +Z.

Base: a heavy round or square plate 150 to 200 mm across, 20 to 40 mm thick, 1 to 3 kg of solid steel or cast aluminium, flat on the desk. It must counterweight the extended arm and shade.

Pivot: a small boss on the base top, 30 to 50 mm high, carries the lower arm on a vertical pin so the whole arm assembly swings side to side.

Arms:
- Lower arm: 300 to 450 mm, a rectangular aluminium section 20 to 30 mm wide and 30 to 40 mm deep, rising 45 to 75 degrees from horizontal at rest.
- Elbow: a round joint 40 to 60 mm across at the lower arm's end.
- Upper arm: 250 to 400 mm, the same section, typically 20 to 60 degrees from horizontal, reaching forward over the desk.

Shade: hangs from the upper arm's end on a short yoke, its mouth 350 to 550 mm above the desk, pointing down at the work; 150 to 250 mm deep.

Springs: one per arm, each running parallel to its arm right beside it, anchored at both ends of the arm segment; 14 to 20 mm coil diameter on 3 to 4 mm wire.

Overall: at rest the lamp stands about 450 to 700 mm tall and reaches 400 to 600 mm forward.
