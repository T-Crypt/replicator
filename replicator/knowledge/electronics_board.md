Orientation: the board lies flat in the XZ plane with components on top (+Y); X is its length, Z its width. Millimetres.

Board and parts (real sizes):
- PCB thickness 1.6 (common), green, black or blue solder mask. Mounting holes for M2.5 or M3 screws about 3 from
  the edges.
- Reference boards: Raspberry Pi 4 is 85 x 56; Arduino Uno is about 68.6 x 53.4.
- Pin headers: 2.54 pitch; the plastic base is about 2.5 tall, pins rise about 6 above it. Rows sit along an edge.
- Chips: SOIC-8 body about 4.9 x 3.9 x 1.5; QFP and QFN microcontrollers about 5 x 5 to 14 x 14, about 1 to 1.6
  tall. Small passives (0805) about 2.0 x 1.25; place them in neat rows near the chip they serve.
- Electrolytic capacitors: cylinders 5 to 10 across, 5 to 12 tall. Crystal: about 11 x 4.5 x 3.5.
- Connectors on the board edge, overhanging slightly: USB-C receptacle about 9 x 3.3 opening, 7 to 8 deep; RJ45
  about 16 x 13.5 x 21; DC barrel jack about 9 x 11 x 14.
- Heat sinks: finned aluminium, fins about 1 thick, 2 to 3 apart.
- Enclosure: walls 2 to 3 thick, 1 to 2 clearance around the board, the board on standoffs 3 to 5 tall; cut-outs in
  the walls line up with the edge connectors.

Every part sits on the board surface (y = board top), never floating above it.
