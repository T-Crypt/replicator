Orientation: the case stands on y = 0. X is its width (the glass side panel faces -X), Z its depth (front panel at +Z).

Standard sizes (millimetres):
- Mid-tower case: about 220 wide, 470 high, 450 deep. Steel panels about 0.8 thick, tempered glass side about 4 thick. Make it hollow with an open or glass side so the build shows.
- Motherboards: ATX 305 x 244, micro-ATX 244 x 244, mini-ITX 170 x 170, all 1.6 thick, mounted on 6 mm standoffs against the right-hand side panel, rear I/O at the top rear.
- Rear I/O opening 159 x 44. Expansion slots at the rear: 7 on ATX, 20.32 apart.
- CPU socket near the top centre of the board; RAM slots (4) just to its right; each DIMM is 133.35 long and about 31 (bare) to 45 (with heat spreader) tall.
- Tower CPU cooler: a finned aluminium block 120 to 165 tall with copper heat pipes and a 120 mm fan on its side. Or an AIO: a pump block on the CPU and a radiator (120, 240 or 360 long, 27 thick) behind the front or top fans.
- Graphics card: in the top PCIe x16 slot below the CPU, lying horizontally, 240 to 340 long, about 110 to 140 tall, 2 to 3.5 slots thick (40 to 70); 2 or 3 axial fans of 90 to 100 underneath, a backplate on top.
- Power supply (ATX): 150 x 86 x 140 to 180, at the bottom rear, often under a shroud.
- Case fans: 120 x 120 x 25 or 140 x 140 x 25; usually three intake fans at the front, one exhaust at the rear.
- Storage: M.2 2280 is 22 x 80 on the board; a 2.5 inch SSD is 100 x 70 x 7.

Building it: use `grille` for vents and fan grilles, `rotor` (hub_radius about 20, tip_radius 58, 7 to 9 blades) for fans with a square frame (box minus cylinder) around them, `box` for boards and cards, thin boxes for fins arrayed linearly for heat sinks.

Materials: black or white painted steel case, smoked glass panel, green or black PCB, aluminium heat sinks, copper heat pipes, matte black plastic fan frames, RGB emissive strips used sparingly.
