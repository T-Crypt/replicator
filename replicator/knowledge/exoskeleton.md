---
title: Powered exoskeleton

Orientation: the wearer stands along Y, facing +Z, feet on y = 0. Build the frame in a standing pose, symmetric about the x = 0 plane (use a mirror array for the left/right limbs). The frame sits just outside the body, 20 to 40 mm clear of it, and its joint axes line up with the wearer's joints.

Joint heights as a fraction of standing height H (Drillis and Contini): ankle 0.04, knee 0.285, hip 0.53, shoulder 0.82, elbow 0.63, wrist 0.485. Segment lengths: thigh 0.245 H, shank 0.246 H, upper arm 0.186 H, forearm 0.146 H. For H = 1.75 m: knee at about 0.50 m, hip at about 0.93 m, shoulder at about 1.43 m. Hip axes of a frame sit roughly 0.35 to 0.45 m apart; make the links adjustable in length by 5 to 10 %.

Parts, bottom to top:
- Foot plates and ankle joint: a rigid sole under or beside each boot, carrying the load into the ground.
- Shank and thigh links: tubes or flat bars, each with a joint housing at both ends and a cuff or strap pad around the limb.
- Knee actuator: motor, gearbox and encoder in one housing coaxial with the joint, or a linear actuator along the thigh.
- Hip actuators: one coaxial with the hip flexion axis; a second joint for abduction is often passive.
- Pelvis frame and back plate: carries the hip joints, the battery and the controller; a belt or harness ties it to the wearer.
- Shoulder frame (upper-body or full-body suits): a yoke on the back plate with arm links, a hand tool mount or a load-bearing arm.

Actuators: brushless frameless torque motor with a strain-wave (harmonic) gearbox at 50:1 to 120:1, or a quasi-direct-drive motor with a planetary gearbox at 6:1 to 10:1; hydraulic cylinders for heavy loads. Walking needs about 1 N m of joint torque per kg of body mass at hip and ankle.

Materials: Ti-6Al-4V (4430 kg/m3, yield about 880 MPa) for joint housings and brackets; 7075-T6 aluminium (2810 kg/m3) for plates; carbon-fibre tube (about 1600 kg/m3), 25 to 40 mm outside diameter with 2 to 3 mm wall, for the long links.

Battery: a 24 to 48 V lithium-ion pack of 1 to 3 kg low on the back plate, close to the wearer's centre of mass, so it adds no lever arm. Powered lower-limb suits weigh 12 to 25 kg in total.
