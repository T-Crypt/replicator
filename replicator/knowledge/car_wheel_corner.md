Orientation: the wheel spins around its axle, which runs along X for a car facing Z; the tyre, rim and brake disc are `torus` or `tube` or `cylinder` ops with "rotate": [0, 0, 90] so the axis is X. The tyre touches y = 0.

Tyre size reads 205/55 R16: 205 mm section width, sidewall height 55 % of that (113 mm), 16 inch rim (406 mm). Overall diameter = rim + 2 x sidewall = 632 mm. Passenger cars run 600 to 700 mm overall, 175 to 255 mm wide, rims 15 to 19 inch.

From the wheel inward, on the axle line:
- Tyre: rubber, with a tread band and two sidewalls; the section is nearly round at the shoulders.
- Rim: a barrel the width of the tyre minus about 30 mm, with a flange at each edge, and a spoke face (5 to 10 spokes) dished inward.
- Hub flange: bolt circle of 4 or 5 studs, usually 5 x 114.3 mm; M12 x 1.5 studs with 17 or 19 mm hex nuts; centre bore about 60 to 73 mm.
- Brake disc: 280 to 330 mm diameter on a 16 to 18 inch rim, 10 to 12 mm thick solid or 22 to 32 mm ventilated, behind the rim spokes.
- Brake caliper: a C-shaped body straddling the disc at the top or rear, 40 to 60 mm wide, pads on both sides of the disc.
- Wheel bearing and steering knuckle: a cast knuckle holds the bearing behind the hub and carries the caliper, the strut and the lower arm.

Suspension (MacPherson, the common front layout):
- Strut: a damper tube 45 to 55 mm in diameter, with a coil spring 120 to 160 mm across around it, running up and slightly inward from the knuckle to a top mount in the wheel arch.
- Lower control arm: an A- or L-shaped arm from the knuckle to the subframe, two rubber bushings on the car side and a ball joint at the wheel.
- Tie rod from the steering rack to the knuckle, anti-roll bar link to the strut.
- Wheel travel: roughly 150 to 200 mm between full droop and full bump; the wheel is vertical or leans 0.5 to 1.5 degrees (negative camber).
